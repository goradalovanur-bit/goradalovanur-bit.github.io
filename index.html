Anur Qoradalov

Mathematics Student

I came to mathematics sideways, through physics — the differential equations behind oscillating systems and fields were the first proofs I actually cared about. Working backward from those equations to their underlying theory is what pulled me fully into pure math.

Outside of coursework I spend a lot of time translating proofs into pictures and code: LaTeX for write-ups, Manim for animated constructions, and MATLAB, C++, and Python when a problem is easier to understand numerically first. This page is the running record of all of it — solved problems, pop-science notes, code, and recorded explanations.

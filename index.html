About

The short version of how I got here, and what I'm currently spending most of my attention on.

I came to mathematics sideways, through physics — the differential equations behind oscillating systems and fields were the first proofs I actually cared about. Working backward from those equations to their underlying theory is what pulled me fully into pure math.

Outside of coursework I spend a lot of time translating proofs into pictures: LaTeX and TikZ for static figures, Manim for animated constructions, and MATLAB, C++, and Python when a problem is easier to understand numerically or computationally first. This site is the running record of all of it — notes, daily problems, visualizations, and recorded explanations.
